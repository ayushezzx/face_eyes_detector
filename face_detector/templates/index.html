💡 BCA Practical Viva Reference: Haar Cascade Concepts

1. Integral Image Calculation

Allows rapid evaluation of rectangular Haar features in constant time \(O(1)\) regardless of scale, summing pixel values inside any sub-rectangle using 4 array lookups.

2. AdaBoost Feature Selection

Selects the most critical visual features out of 160,000+ candidates, combining multiple weak classifiers into a high-accuracy strong classifier.

3. Cascaded Classifier Architecture

Organizes classifiers in stages. Non-face regions are rejected immediately in stage 1 with minimal computational cost, saving GPU/CPU resources.
